About FoodWise AI

AI-powered food demand forecasting and surplus redistribution platform built to support SDG 2 (Zero Hunger) and SDG 12 (Responsible Consumption and Production).

Our Mission

FoodWise helps food-serving organizations — restaurants, college canteens, hotels, hostels, and catering services — reduce preventable food waste through intelligent demand prediction and connect surplus food with nearby NGOs and food banks.

Technology

Built with Python/Flask, SQLite, scikit-learn ML models, Bootstrap 5, and Chart.js.
